About This Class

Katara built The Tide for members who need movement without punishment — the same fluid discipline she learned bending the oceans of the Southern Water Tribe. Each 45-minute session moves like a current: warm, steady, and impossible to rush. Perfect between heavy earth days or after a brutal fire circuit.

  • Moonlit Wave Warm-Up — joint circles and breath-led spinal waves to unlock hips and shoulders.
  • Tidal Push-Pull Flow — alternating resistance-band rows and presses in continuous rhythm.
  • Glacier Hold Core Series — slow hollow-body and side-plank holds that build deep stability.
  • Ripple Recovery Stretch — long holds and flowing transitions to leave you restored, not wrecked.
