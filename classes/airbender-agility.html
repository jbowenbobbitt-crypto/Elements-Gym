The Gust

Airbender Agility

45 Minutes

About This Class

Aang teaches you to move like the wind — light, playful, and impossible to pin down. The Gust is 45 minutes of agility ladders, balance challenges, and bodyweight flow inspired by Air Nomad training. You'll jump, spin, and land with the grace of a sky bison rider.

  • Sky Bison Jump Drills — box jumps, broad jumps, and reactive hop patterns.
  • Appa Glide Balance Flow — single-leg work on BOSU and balance beams.
  • Staff Spin Agility Ladder — footwork drills with directional changes and quick cuts.
  • Nomad Breath Finisher — bodyweight flow paired with mindful breathing cycles.
