About This Class

Zuko rebuilt himself in fire — and he expects the same from you. The Ember is a 45-minute Fire Nation-style HIIT session: explosive, honest, and relentless. No shortcuts, no excuses — just the kind of heat that forges a new you.

  • Agni Kai Sprint Intervals — treadmill and track sprints with short recovery windows.
  • Ember Rope Waves — battle rope complexes paired with jump squats.
  • Dragon Breath Tabata — 20-on / 10-off rounds of burpees, mountain climbers, and box jumps.
  • Phoenix Rise Metcon — a four-move circuit repeated until the clock runs out.
