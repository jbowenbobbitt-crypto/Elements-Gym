The Battalion

Combat

1 Hour 30 Minutes

About This Class

Sokka runs The Battalion like a Southern Water Tribe war council — loud, organized, and absolutely unforgiving. This 90-minute boot camp covers striking, partner defense, and tactical drills that would make his dad proud. Show up on time, follow orders, and don't complain about the burpees.

  • Southern Water Tribe Drill Formation — synchronized calisthenics and marching patterns to build unit cohesion.
  • Boomerang Strike Combos — pad work combining jabs, crosses, hooks, and defensive slips.
  • Invasion Tactics Partner Defense — two-person scenarios for blocks, counters, and footwork under pressure.
  • Commander's Endurance Gauntlet — a timed circuit of sprints, carries, and bodyweight finishers.
