Kyoshi Guard

Women's Self Defense

1 Hour 30 Minutes

About This Class

Suki leads Kyoshi Guard with the same precision she used commanding the warriors of Kyoshi Island. This 90-minute session teaches real-world self-defense through stances, blocks, strikes, and escapes — using pads and trainers, not metal fans. Open to all women; no experience required, only respect for the craft.

  • Kyoshi Warrior Stance Foundations — footwork, posture, and distance management drills.
  • Fan Block & Strike Patterns — pad work for blocks, palm strikes, and elbow counters.
  • Wrist Escape Progressions — step-by-step releases from common grabs and holds.
  • Suki's Awareness Circuit — scenario drills for boundary-setting, verbal de-escalation, and ground recovery.
