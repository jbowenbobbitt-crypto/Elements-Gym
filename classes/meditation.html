About This Class

Mai's Still Mind is a candlelit 45-minute retreat from noise. Guided breathwork, seated stillness, and mental sharpening without fluff — the same cool precision Mai brings to chaos. Ideal recovery after Blue Lightning or high-stress weeks.

  • Candle Breathe Count — box breathing and 4-7-8 patterns to slow the nervous system.
  • Shadow Stillness Sit — extended seated meditation with posture cues and gentle corrections.
  • Knives-of-Calm Focus Drill — attention training using a single focal point and soft gaze.
  • Mai's Silent Release — body scan and quiet closing ritual before re-entering the world.
