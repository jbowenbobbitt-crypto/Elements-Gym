The Stone

Earthbender Grind

45 Minutes

About This Class

Toph doesn't care about flashy reps — she cares that you stay grounded. The Stone is 45 minutes of uncompromising strength work modeled on earthbending's immovable stance: heavy loads, long holds, and zero wobble. You'll feel like you could stop a platypus bear in its tracks by the end.

  • Badgermole Burrow Squats — tempo back squats with pause reps at the bottom.
  • Granite Deadlift Ladder — ascending deadlift sets that build raw pulling power.
  • Seismic Hold Challenge — farmer carries and front-rack holds for grip and core.
  • Blind Bandit Finisher — sled pushes and sandbag carries until the floor shakes.
