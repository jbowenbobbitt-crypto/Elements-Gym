Usage Risks

Staff Flow Bars

Aang's Sky Deck · Air · Agility & Balance

Primary Risks

  • Facial and finger impact — lost control during spins can strike the face, knuckles, or nearby members.
  • Wrist sprains — catching a staff wrong on transitions overloads the wrist joint.
  • Splintering wood — cracked practice staffs can splinter mid-drill and cut hands.
  • Collision in shared space — staff work requires clearance; spinning into another member causes serious injury.

How to Train Safely

  • Maintain a clear radius — Aang's staff drills need open floor space on all sides.
  • Inspect staffs before use — retire any bar with cracks, splinters, or loose ends.
  • Start slow on new patterns — build spin control before speed in The Gust.
