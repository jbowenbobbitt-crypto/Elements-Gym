Usage Risks

Metal Grip Stations

Toph's Iron Lot · Earth · Strength & Power

Primary Risks

  • Elbow tendinopathy — plate pinches and thick-bar work without progression can inflame tendons in the forearm and elbow.
  • Fall from pull-up failure — grip giving out on thick-bar pull-ups can mean an uncontrolled drop from height.
  • Lower back strain on carries — farmer walks with uneven loads or leaning posture loads the spine asymmetrically.
  • Callus tears — heavy grip work can rip palm skin if calluses are overgrown or chalk is excessive.

How to Train Safely

  • Build grip volume gradually — Toph's metal control takes time; don't max pinches on day one.
  • Use crash mats below bars — position padding under pull-up stations for failed reps.
  • Keep shoulders packed on carries — stand tall and square when moving farmer handles.
