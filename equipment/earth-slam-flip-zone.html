Usage Risks

Earth Slam & Flip Zone

Toph's Iron Lot · Earth · Strength & Power

Primary Risks

  • Bicep tear on tire flips — curling a heavy tire under the lip instead of driving with legs and hips can snap the bicep tendon.
  • Rebound injury from slam balls — slam balls that bounce back toward the face can cause head or jaw impact.
  • Foot and ankle crush — a tire rolling back during a failed flip can land on feet if the flip zone isn't cleared.
  • Spinal compression on sandbag lifts — awkward picks with a rounded back under shifting sand loads the lumbar spine.

How to Train Safely

  • Leg drive on flips — treat tire flips like a deadlift, not an arm curl.
  • Use non-rebound slam balls only — confirm ball type before overhead slams.
  • Clear the flip lane — keep the zone empty behind you before every rep.
