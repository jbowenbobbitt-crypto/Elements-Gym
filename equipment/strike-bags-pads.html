Usage Risks

Strike Bags & Pads

Zuko's Ember Circuit · Fire · HIIT & Conditioning

Primary Risks

  • Hand and wrist fractures — striking with poor fist alignment on heavy bags can break metacarpals.
  • Shoulder strain from overuse — high-rep combos in The Ember without rest inflames rotator cuffs.
  • Partner pad injuries — mistimed mitt work can jam the holder's elbow or shoulder on incoming force.
  • Loose bag swing — an improperly chained heavy bag can swing into the striker's face or body.

How to Train Safely

  • Wrap hands every session — use wraps and gloves before any bag or pad work.
  • Check bag chains and mounts — confirm hardware is secure before fire rounds begin.
  • Communicate with pad partners — call combos clearly and reset stance between flurries.
