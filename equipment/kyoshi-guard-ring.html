Usage Risks

Kyoshi Guard Training Ring

Elite Programs · Suki's Command Floor · Women's Self Defense

Primary Risks

  • Pad holder finger injuries — fan-style block pads can jam fingers if grip loosens on hard strikes.
  • Wrist strain on escapes — repetitive wrist-escape drills against resistance can inflame tendons.
  • Stance rail trips — low stance rails used for footwork can catch toes during fast pivots.
  • Contact intensity mismatch — partners striking too hard during pad work cause bruising and loss of trust.

How to Train Safely

  • Agree on contact level — Suki's ring runs on respect; check in with partners before drills.
  • Secure pad grips — hold block trainers firmly with proper thumb placement.
  • Progress escape speed slowly — build wrist-escape technique before adding resistance.
