Usage Risks

Still Mind Meditation Alcove

Yoga & Meditation · Mai's Wellness Studio · Still Mind

Primary Risks

  • Knee and hip discomfort — prolonged sitting on thin cushions without proper elevation strains knees and hips.
  • Lower back pain from poor posture — slouching on zafu cushions for 45 minutes loads the lumbar spine.
  • Fire hazard from candles — lit candles in the alcove require supervision and clearance from cushions and fabric.
  • Dizziness from breathwork — aggressive breath counts without guidance can cause lightheadedness or hyperventilation.

How to Train Safely

  • Elevate hips on cushions — stack zafu and zabuton so knees rest below hip level.
  • Never leave candles unattended — extinguish all flames after Mai's sessions.
  • Return to normal breathing if dizzy — stop breathwork drills and sit quietly until balance returns.
