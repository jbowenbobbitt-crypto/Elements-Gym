Usage Risks

Acrobat Flexibility Rig

Yoga & Meditation · Ty Lee's Wellness Studio · Blossom Flow

Primary Risks

  • Fall from silk hammocks — inverted or wrapped poses without proper setup can mean uncontrolled drops.
  • Shoulder strain on low rings — hanging or swinging on rings without shoulder engagement impinges the rotator cuff.
  • Overstretch on stretch bars — using bars to pull into splits beyond capacity can tear hip flexors or hamstrings.
  • Equipment mount failure — loose rigging on hammocks or rings creates sudden collapse risk.

How to Train Safely

  • Inspect rigging daily — confirm mounts and carabiners before Ty Lee's acrobatic progressions.
  • Spot inversions initially — have a coach nearby when learning hammock or ring work.
  • Build range gradually — circus flexibility takes months; don't force the splits bar on week one.
