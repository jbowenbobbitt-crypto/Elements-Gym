Usage Risks

Plyo & Sled Lane

Zuko's Ember Circuit · Fire · HIIT & Conditioning

Primary Risks

  • ACL and ankle sprains — box jumps with poor landing mechanics or box heights beyond skill level are a top injury source.
  • Shin splints from sled sprints — repeated high-force sled pushes on hard surfaces can inflame shins and calves.
  • Box edge impact — catching a shin on the plyo box edge on a missed jump causes deep bruising or fractures.
  • Lower back strain on heavy sleds — pushing with a rounded spine under load mimics bad firebending form and strains the lumbar spine.

How to Train Safely

  • Land soft, knees tracking toes — absorb impact through hips on every box jump.
  • Step down, don't jump off boxes — preserve joints between Ember rounds.
  • Progress sled load slowly — add weight only when sprint form stays sharp.
