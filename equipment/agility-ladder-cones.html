Usage Risks

Agility Ladder & Cones

Aang's Sky Deck · Air · Agility & Balance

Primary Risks

  • Ankle rolls — catching a rung edge or cone during fast footwork can invert the ankle.
  • Trips on loose ladder rungs — poorly secured ladders shift mid-drill and cause falls.
  • Knee valgus on cuts — sharp direction changes with knees collapsing inward stress ACL integrity.
  • Slip on wet floor — high-speed ladder work on slick surfaces after sweat buildup increases fall risk.

How to Train Safely

  • Anchor the ladder flat — confirm rungs lie flush before Air Nomad footwork drills.
  • Build speed gradually — nail pattern accuracy before matching Aang's pace.
  • Wipe the lane if slick — dry the floor before cone sprints and ladder work.
