Usage Risks

Recovery & Mobility Kits

Katara's Training Floor · Water · Flow & Recovery

Primary Risks

  • Nerve compression — aggressive foam rolling over the spine, neck, or joints can compress nerves instead of releasing muscle.
  • Aggravated inflammation — rolling acute injuries or fresh strains can increase swelling and delay healing.
  • Over-stretching — stretch straps used to force range beyond capacity can tear hamstrings or hip flexors.
  • Hygiene exposure — shared massage balls and rollers can spread skin irritation if not cleaned between users.

How to Train Safely

  • Roll muscle, not bone — stay on soft tissue; avoid spine, knees, and bony landmarks.
  • Skip acute pain — if an area is freshly injured, use ice and consult a coach before rolling.
  • Light pressure first — Katara's healing work is gentle; increase depth only when tissue relaxes.
