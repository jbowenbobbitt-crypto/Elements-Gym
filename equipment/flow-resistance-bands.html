Usage Risks

Flow Resistance Bands

Katara's Training Floor · Water · Flow & Recovery

Primary Risks

  • Snap-back injury — a worn or overstretched band can recoil into face, eyes, or joints at full tension.
  • Anchor failure — improper door anchors or loose posts can release mid-rep and cause a fall.
  • Shoulder hyperextension — circular arm paths with too much band tension can strain the rear deltoid and rotator cuff.
  • Skin pinching — bands caught against bare skin during hip openers can pinch or abrade.

How to Train Safely

  • Inspect before use — replace bands with cracks, fraying, or discoloration immediately.
  • Secure anchors — test anchor points with light tension before full water-flow drills.
  • Progress resistance slowly — move to heavier bands only when form stays fluid through full range.
