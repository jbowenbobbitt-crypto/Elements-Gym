Usage Risks

Balance & Rings Rig

Aang's Sky Deck · Air · Agility & Balance

Primary Risks

  • Fall from height on rings — failed muscle-ups, dips, or inversions can mean uncontrolled drops from the rig.
  • Ankle instability on BOSU — standing on the dome with poor balance can roll the ankle off the edge.
  • Beam impact injuries — losing balance on a narrow beam leads to hard lateral falls onto the floor or equipment.
  • Shoulder strain on hangs — hanging or swinging on rings without shoulder engagement can impinge the rotator cuff.

How to Train Safely

  • Use mats under the rig — place crash padding beneath rings and beam work.
  • Pack shoulders before hangs — engage lats and scapula before any ring or inversion drill.
  • Spot BOSU work initially — have a coach nearby when learning airbender balance progressions.
