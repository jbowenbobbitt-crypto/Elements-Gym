Master's Equipment

Every station is built around how our four coaches train — fluid like Katara, grounded like Toph, relentless like Zuko, and light as Aang.

Water · Flow & Recovery

Katara's Training Floor

Katara trains with continuous motion — pull, redirect, and heal. Her floor favors resistance that flows with your body, not against it.

🌊

Tidal Rowers

Rowing machines for long, pulling strokes — mimicking waterbending draw and release cycles in The Tide.

💧

Flow Resistance Bands

Anchor bands for circular arm paths and hip openers — the same redirecting drills Katara uses to warm up the spine.

🩹

Recovery & Mobility Kits

Foam rollers, massage balls, and stretch straps for healing-focused cooldowns after every water session.

Earth · Strength & Power

Toph's Iron Lot

Toph trains by feeling the ground — heavy loads, grip work, and explosive power rooted through your feet. Nothing fancy; everything solid.

🪨

Seismic Deadlift Platforms

Reinforced platforms and trap bars for deadlifts and carries — stomp the floor and drive from the heels like The Stone.

⚙️

Metal Grip Stations

Farmer handles, thick-bar pull-ups, and plate pinches — grip strength drills inspired by Toph's metalbending control.

🛞

Earth Slam & Flip Zone

Tire flips, sandbags, and slam balls for raw power — feel the impact travel through the ground on every rep.

Fire · HIIT & Conditioning

Zuko's Ember Circuit

Zuko trains with discipline and fire — sharp strikes, nonstop rounds, and the mindset to push through when your body wants to quit.

🥊

Strike Bags & Pads

Heavy bags and focus mitts for precise, repeated strikes — the same relentless combos drilled in The Ember.

🔥

Battle Rope Bay

Wave ropes and interval timers for explosive upper-body bursts — channel short, intense firebending bursts.

📦

Plyo & Sled Lane

Plyo boxes and push sleds for forward drive and jump power — Zuko's redemption runs, rep after rep.

Air · Agility & Balance

Aang's Sky Deck

Aang trains light on his feet — staff flow, leaps, and balance work that keeps you moving like the wind instead of fighting it.

🪄

Staff Flow Bars

Wooden practice staffs and PVC guides for spins, blocks, and transitions — core drills from The Gust.

🪜

Agility Ladder & Cones

Footwork ladders and marker cones for quick direction changes — the light-footed patterns Aang runs daily.

🎪

Balance & Rings Rig

BOSU balls, balance beams, and gymnastic rings for airbender-style control, hangs, and inversions.

Not sure where to start? Match your element class to the coach's floor, or ask any coach on the floor — refuel afterward at the café with Iroh's guidance.
