Classes

Nine signature sessions — elemental training, elite programs, and mind-body practice with Ty Lee and Mai. Master one discipline or challenge yourself across the full roster.

Element Classes

💧

The Tide

Waterbender Flow

Fluid mobility and low-impact cardio inspired by Water Tribe movement — smooth transitions, deep stretches, and rhythmic flow work.

Coach: Katara · Focus: Flexibility · Recovery · Endurance

🪨

The Stone

Earthbender Grind

Heavy strength and stability training — squats, deadlifts, and holds that build an unshakeable foundation like the Earth Kingdom.

Coach: Toph · Focus: Strength · Power · Core

🔥

The Ember

Firebender Burn

High-intensity intervals and explosive circuits — channel Fire Nation energy with sprints, battle ropes, and metabolic finishers.

Coach: Zuko · Focus: HIIT · Speed · Fat Burn

🌪️

The Gust

Airbender Agility

Light-footed agility, balance drills, and bodyweight flow — move like an Air Nomad with jumps, spins, and mindful breathwork.

Coach: Aang · Focus: Agility · Balance · Mobility

Elite Programs

⚔️

The Battalion

Combat

Sokka's military-style boot camp — drill formations, partner defense, striking combos, and weapon-footwork patterns modeled after Southern Water Tribe warrior training. Expect orders, timed sets, and zero excuses.

Coach: Sokka · Focus: Striking · Tactics · Team Drills · Discipline

⚡

Maximum Intensity

Blue Lightning

1 Hour · All Out

Azula's signature nightmare session — one brutal hour of nonstop full-body cardio and muscle work. Burpees into sled pushes, kettlebell complexes, battle ropes, and bodyweight burners stacked back-to-back with almost no rest. Not for beginners. Not for the faint of heart.

Coach: Azula · Duration: 60 min · Focus: Full Body · Cardio · Strength · Endurance

🪭

Kyoshi Guard

Women's Self Defense

Train like a Kyoshi Warrior under Suki's instruction — precise stances, fan-style blocks and strikes (using pads and trainers, not metal fans), wrist escapes, and ground recovery. Built for real-world awareness: reading distance, setting boundaries, and defending with the same discipline Suki used leading the warriors of Kyoshi Island. Open to all women; no experience required — only respect for the craft and for each other.

Coach: Suki · Focus: Strikes · Blocks · Escapes · Confidence · Situational Awareness

Yoga & Meditation

🌸

Blossom Flow

Yoga

Ty Lee's signature session — light, acrobatic yoga in a blossom-themed studio. Flow through sun salutations, hip openers, and playful balance poses drawn from circus training and chi-blocking flexibility. Energy is bright and encouraging; mats feel like petals underfoot. Perfect after heavy earth days or before air agility work.

Coach: Ty Lee · Focus: Flexibility · Balance · Flow · Joyful Movement

🌑

Still Mind

Meditation

Mai's candlelit meditation — a dark, quiet room where distractions fall away. Guided breathwork, seated stillness, and mental sharpening without fluff. Train the mind to stay cool under pressure, the way Mai faces chaos with a single exhale. Ideal recovery after Blue Lightning or high-stress weeks.

Coach: Mai · Focus: Breathwork · Stillness · Focus · Stress Relief

New to the gym? Start with an element class, explore Combat or Blue Lightning, or reset with Ty Lee's Yoga and Mai's Meditation. See gym hours and meet the coaches on our team page.
