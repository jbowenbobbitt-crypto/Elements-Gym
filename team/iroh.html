Uncle Iroh

Wise tea master, keeper of the café, and believer that strength comes from balance — not just the gym floor.

Who He Is

Once Dragon of the West, now Dragon of the Jasmine Leaf — Iroh runs The Jasmine Dragon Café inside Element Masters Gym and offers personal nutrition consultations for members who want food and drink working with their training. He maps plates to class schedules, builds practical meal plans without extreme diets, and always has time for a cup of jasmine tea and honest advice.

Services Offered

  • Custom Meal Plan — macros and meals tailored to your class load and goals.
  • Hydration Strategy — pre- and post-workout drinking plans paired with café options.
  • Goal Alignment — fat loss, muscle gain, or maintenance without fad diets.
  • Café Coaching — a personal guide to The Jasmine Dragon menu for every training day.
  • Follow-Up Check-In — plan adjustments as your element training evolves.
