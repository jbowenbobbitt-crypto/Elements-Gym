Who She Is

Ty Lee traded the circus tent for a blossom-themed studio at Element Masters Gym. Her sessions blend sun salutations, hip openers, and playful balance poses drawn from acrobatic training and chi-blocking flexibility. The energy is encouraging and light — perfect after heavy earth days or before air agility work.

Services Offered

  • Blossom Flow Yoga — signature group class with flow sequences and balance challenges.
  • Flexibility Intensive — targeted stretching for hips, shoulders, and spine.
  • Acro-Yoga Intro — partner-based poses for members with base experience.
  • Recovery Flow — gentle yoga sessions after high-intensity training weeks.
