Who He Is

Sokka may not bend elements, but he bends groups into teams. A self-appointed tactician and proud non-bender, he runs Combat class like a war camp: drill formations, partner defense, striking combos, and weapon-footwork patterns modeled after Water Tribe warrior training. Expect orders, timed sets, and absolutely zero excuses.

Services Offered

  • Combat Boot Camp — group military-style sessions with partner drills and team challenges.
  • Striking Fundamentals — pad work, combos, and defensive footwork for all levels.
  • Tactical Team Training — small-group sessions focused on coordination and communication.
  • Strategy Sessions — workout planning for members who want a battle plan, not guesswork.
