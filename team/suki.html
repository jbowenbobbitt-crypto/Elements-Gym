Suki

Captain of the Kyoshi Warriors — precise stances, real-world awareness, and unshakeable confidence.

Who She Is

Leader of the Kyoshi Warriors, Suki brings battlefield discipline to the gym floor. Her program teaches fan-style blocks and strikes (using pads and trainers, not metal fans), wrist escapes, ground recovery, and situational awareness. Open to all women; no experience required — only respect for the craft and for each other.

Services Offered

  • Kyoshi Guard Class — group women's self-defense with stances, strikes, and escapes.
  • Private Defense Coaching — one-on-one sessions tailored to personal safety goals.
  • Confidence & Awareness Workshop — boundary-setting, distance reading, and de-escalation.
  • Warrior Conditioning — strength and agility work specific to self-defense scenarios.
