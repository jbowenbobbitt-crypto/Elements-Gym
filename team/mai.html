Mai

Candlelit stillness, zero fluff — train the mind to stay cool when everything else is chaos.

Who She Is

Mai runs the gym's darkest, quietest room. Her meditation sessions use guided breathwork, seated stillness, and mental sharpening without motivational speeches. She teaches members to face pressure with a single exhale — ideal recovery after Blue Lightning or high-stress weeks. If Ty Lee is the blossom, Mai is the shadow that lets it rest.

Services Offered

  • Still Mind Meditation — signature group session in a dim, distraction-free studio.
  • Breathwork Coaching — techniques for focus, calm, and nervous-system recovery.
  • Stress Relief Sessions — short guided sits for members between intense training blocks.
  • Private Meditation — one-on-one instruction for members new to stillness practice.
