Who He Is

Marked by the arrow tattoo that guides Element Masters Gym's philosophy, Aang brings Air Nomad principles to every session: agility without ego, balance without rigidity, and breath as the foundation of all movement. His classes feel playful but precise — jumps, spins, bodyweight flow, and mindful footwork that make members feel like they're flying without leaving the floor.
