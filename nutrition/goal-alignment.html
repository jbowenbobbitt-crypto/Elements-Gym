What You'll Get

Goal Alignment

Fat loss, muscle gain, or maintenance — mapped to your element training without extreme diets.

What It Includes

Iroh believes balance beats deprivation. Your nutrition goal is tied directly to the classes on your calendar — not a crash diet that collapses the moment Azula adds another burpee circuit.

  • Goal-specific calorie targets — modest deficits for fat loss, surpluses for muscle gain, maintenance for performance.
  • Training-phase mapping — how to eat during heavy earth blocks vs. recovery weeks with yoga and meditation.
  • Progress check-ins — adjust macros as your strength, endurance, or body composition shifts.
  • Sustainable habits — no banned foods; Iroh teaches portion wisdom and timing instead.

Who It's For

Members with a clear target — drop weight for agility work, add muscle for The Stone, or maintain while surviving elite programs — who want a plan that respects both the gym floor and the dinner table.
